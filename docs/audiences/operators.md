# AI Product & Operations Teams

AgentFlyer is strong when you need operator control, auditable execution, and durable outputs across repeated AI processes.

## Typical scenarios

- Intake → research → review → publish pipelines.
- Human approvals for high-impact tool calls.
- Scheduler-triggered recurring tasks with execution history.

## What success looks like

- Operators can inspect session state and pending approvals at runtime.
- Repeated work is moved from ad hoc chat into workflows.
- Outputs are tracked as deliverables, not lost in chat history.

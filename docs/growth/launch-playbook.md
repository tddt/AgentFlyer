# 30-Day Launch Playbook

This page turns the growth plan into repository-ready assets and execution checklists.

## North Star (Track only 3 metrics)

- **UV**: unique visitors (daily / weekly)
- **Conversion**: Star, install, signup (choose one primary conversion and one secondary)
- **Retention**: 7-day repeat visitors or 7-day active users

## Week 1 — Positioning and Foundation

- Day 1: finalize one-line positioning and target persona
- Day 2: lock 3 core scenarios with result-oriented one-liners
- Day 3: optimize homepage title, value proposition, CTA
- Day 4: improve README 5-minute quick start + FAQ
- Day 5: publish one intro article
- Day 6: establish analytics baseline and UTM naming
- Day 7: weekly retro and Week 2 channel focus

Deliverables:

- One-line positioning and scenario copy in README
- 5-minute quick start and FAQ in README
- Intro article draft from template
- Analytics baseline sheet and weekly retro template

## Week 2 — First Distribution Round

- Day 8: publish first platform post in 2 channels
- Day 9: publish 60-second demo GIF/video
- Day 10: contribute practical, non-sales posts in 3 communities
- Day 11: publish objective comparison article
- Day 12: contact 5 potential users for 1:1 trial interviews
- Day 13: update homepage/README copy using feedback
- Day 14: channel performance review, keep top 2 channels

## Week 3 — Content Consistency and Social Proof

- Day 15: publish hands-on tutorial #1 (0→1)
- Day 16: publish 3 real usage cases
- Day 17: publish pitfalls and best practices
- Day 18: ongoing community replies and Q&A
- Day 19: collect early testimonials
- Day 20: move recurring questions into docs/examples
- Day 21: review best-performing content format

## Week 4 — Conversion Optimization and Amplification

- Day 22: optimize conversion path (visit → trial / star / join)
- Day 23: unify CTA in homepage, README, and posts
- Day 24: publish advanced tutorial #2
- Day 25: run one cross-promo with a complementary project
- Day 26: re-engage prior visitors with release/update post
- Day 27: follow up high-intent users
- Day 28: consolidate monthly data and conclusions
- Day 29: define next-month experiments (2 measurable hypotheses)
- Day 30: publish monthly report
